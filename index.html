Fit first

Read measurements, not just labels.

For a sweatshirt or oversized T-shirt, compare chest width and body length with a garment you like. Check whether “chest” means a flat width or a full circumference. Keep centimeters and inches clearly separated.

For slides, ask whether a stated length describes the foot, insole or outsole. A size label alone does not establish fit. Record the seller’s advice separately from measurements you have confirmed.

Before parcel approval

A practical QC checklist

  • Match the product, color, quantity and size to your order.
  • Look for visible damage, uneven stitching and missing parts.
  • Request a specific measurement when fit is uncertain.
  • Record concerns and check the order’s return conditions promptly.
  • Confirm packed dimensions and the shipping line’s charging rules.

Photographs help assess visible details; they do not independently prove material composition, authenticity or durability.

A little more clarity

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds, source links, prices, selected options and order notes. This independent page combines CAFANS catalog references with comparison tools.

Where do the buttons take me?

The LitBuy spreadsheet and Buy it now buttons open CAFANS. This page does not accept payments or automatically create a LitBuy order.

Are the displayed prices delivered prices?

No. They are estimated USD item prices recorded from the CAFANS homepage on 19 November 2026. Shipping, fees and taxes are not included, and current options may have different prices.

Are catalog photos quality-control photos?

No. These are listing images from CAFANS-linked product records. Review photographs of your own warehouse order and confirm measurements before shipping.

Does every CAFANS item work with LitBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified for every item. Obtain the original marketplace listing and check it on your chosen agent’s platform. Confirm the available options, purchasing restrictions and current terms there.

How current is this selection?

The three source records and homepage prices were checked on 19 November 2026. This date describes the reference import, not an inspection, stock guarantee or continuous price update.

Can I keep a spreadsheet copy?

Yes. Export CSV downloads the currently filtered records with source URLs, estimated prices and a checked date. Add your selected options and confirmed order costs in your own spreadsheet.
